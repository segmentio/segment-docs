[Attribution](https://attributionapp.com/?utm_source=segmentio&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=partners){:target="_blank"} is a mobile
and web attribution platform that helps marketers measure and optimize the performance of their marketing campaigns.

This destination is maintained by Attribution. For any issues with the destination, [contact their Support
team](mailto:support@attributionapp.com).

## Getting started

1. From your workspace's [Destination catalog page](https://app.segment.com/goto-my-workspace/destinations/catalog){:target="_blank"}
search for "Attribution".
2. Select **Attribution (Actions)** and click **Add Destination**.
3. Select an existing Source to connect to Attribution (Actions).
4. Go to your [Attribution dashboard](https://app.attributionapp.com){:target="_blank"} and locate your **Project ID**.
5. Enter the **Project ID** in the Attribution (Actions) destination settings in Segment.
