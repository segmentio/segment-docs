[Qualified](https://qualified.com/?utm_source=segmentio&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=partners){:target="\_blank”} is the Agentic Marketing Platform for B2B companies around the world. With Piper the AI SDR Agent, Qualified offers a whole new way to grow inbound pipeline. Piper manages the entire inbound marketing funnel, across both the website and email inbox, working to capture leads, nurture prospects, and convert buyers into pipeline around the clock. Hundreds of the world’s leading brands—including Crunchbase, Asana, Box, and Grubhub—choose Qualified to increase lead conversions, generate more meetings, and improve efficiency within their inbound pipeline motion.

This destination is maintained by Qualified. For any issues with the destination, [contact their Support team](mailto:help@qualified.com).

**Getting started**

1. From your workspace's [Destination catalog page](https://app.segment.com/goto-my-workspace/destinations/catalog){:target="\_blank”} search for "Qualified".
2. Select Qualified and click **Add Destination**.
3. Select an existing Source to connect to Qualified.
4. Go to your [Qualified API settings](https://app.qualified.com/settings/api-keys){:target="\_blank"}, create and copy the **API key**.
5. Enter the **API Key** in the destination settings in Segment.
