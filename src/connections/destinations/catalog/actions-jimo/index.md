[Jimo](https://jimo.ai/?utm_source=segmentio&utm_medium=docs&utm_campaign=partners){:target="\_blank”} lets you create powerful in-app experiences—such as AI Copilots, onboarding tours, marketing modals, hints, and more, improving your product adoption and without needing to involve your developers.

This destination is maintained by Jimo. For any issues with the destination, [contact their Support team](mailto:support@jimo.ai).

## Getting started

1. From your workspace's [Destination catalog page](https://app.segment.com/goto-my-workspace/destinations/catalog){:target="\_blank”} search for "Jimo Cloud (Action)".
2. Select Jimo Cloud and click **Add Destination**.
3. Select an existing Source to connect to Jimo Cloud (Actions).
4. Go to the [Jimo dashboard](https://i.usejimo.com/settings/integrations/segmentcloud){:target="\_blank"}, find and copy the **API key**.
5. Enter the **API Key** in the Jimo Cloud destination settings in Segment.
