[Collab Travel CRM](https://collabtravelcrm.com) is the all-in-one platform for modern travel agencies. This integration allows you to sync your bookings, leads, and agent activity directly with your marketing and analytics tools.

## Getting Started

1. From your Segment UI's Destinations page click on "Add Destination".
2. Search for "Collab Travel CRM" in the Catalog, select it, and choose which of your sources to connect the destination to.
3. Navigate to the **Settings** > **Integrations** page in your Collab Travel CRM dashboard to generate your **API Key**.
4. Enter the **API Key** into the connection settings in Segment.

## Supported Events

This destination supports the following events:

* **Trip Booked**: Sent when a booking is confirmed. Includes revenue, currency, and destination details.
* **Lead Created**: Sent when a new client profile is created.
* **Itinerary Sent**: Sent when an agent shares a proposal.
